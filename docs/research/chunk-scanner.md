# Native chunk scan scheduler

**Status:** The C++ scheduler works with a fixture reader and compiles as part of the ARM64 module target. No live adapter invokes it in the LeviPack. The HUD still shows synthetic geometry only. The [passing one-block device probe](one-block-world-probe.md#device-result) and the separate [Loaded Diamond Scan trial](loaded-diamond-scan.md) use the supported Script API; neither feeds or verifies the native scheduler.

## Scan contract

1. The update adapter supplies a copied world epoch/dimension, player position, inclusive build heights, and a complete list of client **render-ready sections** for the scan window. Render-ready means the client has the section available for terrain rendering, not that its mesh was drawn this frame. Use block data, not the visible faces. A saved-world, server-loaded, or mesh-build-start list is not equivalent.
2. [`ChunkScanner`](../../include/ore_outline/chunk_scan.hpp) intersects the ready feed with the section window around the player's current section. A section is a logical 16×16×16 grid. The default radius is one section each way on each axis; horizontal and vertical radii are capped at four and three, respectively. The adapter prefilters to at most 1,024 input entries; oversized or ambiguous in-range feeds cause a clear with no reads. Zero stamps are unknown. The maximum in-window set is 567 sections, with reads clipped to the dimension's valid Y range.
3. Process nearest sections first and resume the per-section cursor over successive updates. The default allowance is 256 block read attempts per update, hard-capped at 1,024. Check the section's current stamp and availability again before every read. The reader must keep the world and section valid through the copy and must never use a load/request/generation path. A false gate or failed copy ends work for that section in the current update and drops its outlines. `nullopt` is not air. Completed, unchanged sections need readiness checks but no block reads.
4. Process changes through section content stamps. A new stamp drops old outlines and queues the affected section again. The stamp must cover hidden block edits and reloads, not just mesh rebuilds. Changing filters restarts discovery. Sections leaving the window or ready feed are dropped immediately; disabling the mod, losing context, or changing worlds/dimensions clears everything. The cache keeps at most 256 matching positions. Only copied positions and categories go to the HUD; building the camera snapshot and synchronizing publication is the adapter's responsibility.

All of this runs on one verified owning update path, **not** inside HUD drawing or a terrain render-layer callback. The budgets bound calls, not wall time: they are starting limits for device measurement. The guard prepass also rechecks completed sections so an outdated render-feed entry alone cannot keep an outline alive.

## Validation so far

`make test` discovers two ores in neighboring fixture chunks (`x = −1` and `x = 0` in chunk units), with neither ore's block coordinates passed to the scheduler. A reversed feed still scans the player's section first. The test covers 8,192 positions over 34 updates of at most 243 reads, then zero reads on an unchanged update. A data-present but non-render-ready ore and an out-of-range ore are not read. Other cases exercise stone/air replacement, new ore, content stamp changes, range/filter invalidation, zero reads with a failed gate, loss of readiness mid-scan, world changes, invalid input, integer limits and vertical clipping, and hard read/cache limits. The copied result projects to two boxes with a synthetic camera. This is host evidence, not device timing or a native world read.

The [passive lifecycle investigation](render-section-lifecycle.md) identifies distinct shared geometry and view-grid update paths in the matching ELF. It does not validate a live feed or an existing-only reader. Leave live scanning off until those contracts are established.

Then place ore at two positions not supplied to the mod in neighboring render-ready chunks. Require both to be discovered by scanning, no attempts in a non-ready control, and correct replacement/removal on edits and when moving out of range or leaving the world. Record read counts and update cost; verify coherent camera/viewport separately before drawing on real terrain. No extra install or test on the device is requested for this host-only milestone.
